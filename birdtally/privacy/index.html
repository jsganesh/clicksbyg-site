BirdTally Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

BirdTally is a bird notebook for phones, tablets and computers. It is available as a web app and, in testing, as an iPhone and iPad app. This policy covers both. In short: your notebook stays on your device unless you send or copy it somewhere yourself. BirdTally collects no data about you.

Data we do not collect

BirdTally does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. Your sightings, notes, voice notes, photos, locations and checklists are stored on your device. They leave it only in the ways described under "Copies you make" below, and each of those is something you start.

Location

When you add a bird or turn on Birding mode, BirdTally asks your device for one position and saves it with that entry, so your checklist can say where you were. It does not track you continuously or in the background, and it never sends your location anywhere. You are asked for permission the first time; if you decline, BirdTally still works and entries are saved without a position.

Microphone, camera and photos

BirdTally uses the microphone only while you record a voice note, and the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo. Voice notes and photos are stored on your device with the entry. BirdTally never uploads them. You are asked for permission the first time you use each feature.

Internet connections

Recording birds needs no connection. BirdTally connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • Species names: when you tap Download in Setup, BirdTally downloads the official eBird species list from the eBird API (api.ebird.org, run by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology). The request contains only the languages you chose. Nothing from your notebook is sent.
  • Regional species list (optional): if you enter your own eBird API key, BirdTally sends that key and the trip's country code to the eBird API to download the species reported there. The key is stored only on your device.
  • The web app itself: the web version is served by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address when the app is loaded or updated. GitHub's privacy statement covers that.
  • Links you open: links to eBird or help pages open in your browser.

The Cornell Lab's privacy policy covers requests to the eBird API. BirdTally is not affiliated with the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Analytics, tracking and third parties

BirdTally contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and shares nothing with third parties. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you test the iPhone app through TestFlight, Apple shares the name and email address you were invited with, and any feedback, screenshots or crash reports you choose to send, with the developer. Apple may also collect crash reports and usage data if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device's settings. Apple's privacy policy covers that data.

Copies you make

BirdTally has no server. A copy of your data leaves the app only when you make one:

  • Backups and trip files: a backup file holds your whole notebook: sightings, notes, locations, voice notes, photos, trips and checklists. You choose where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, Mail and so on). After that, the file is governed by the place you sent it to.
  • eBird files: an eBird import file holds the checklists you selected: species, counts, comments, locations, dates and times. When you upload it to eBird, eBird's terms and privacy policy apply, and your checklists may become public on eBird according to your eBird settings.
  • Copying an entry sheet: the text goes to the app you paste it into.

Your data, your control

  • Deletion: you can delete any sighting in the app. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data in your browser, removes the data BirdTally keeps on that device.
  • Copies stay where you put them: deleting a sighting, or the app, does not delete backups or eBird files you saved elsewhere, or checklists you uploaded to eBird. Delete those where they are kept.

Children

BirdTally is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. If a change affects what BirdTally does with your data, the new policy will be published before the version that makes the change.
