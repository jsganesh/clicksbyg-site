BirdTally

An offline bird notebook for photographers.

Log birds in two or three taps while you photograph, with no signal and no account. Each entry keeps its time and place. Back at base, BirdTally groups your entries into birding sessions and turns them into files you upload to eBird yourself.

Open BirdTally

Free. Early release. BirdTally is a web app: open it once while online, then add it to your Home Screen (iPhone and iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: ⋮ → Install app). After that it works with no connection.

What it does

  • Add a bird by tapping a recent species, or typing a few letters of its name or code
  • Names in English, te reo Māori and other languages, from the official eBird taxonomy
  • Voice notes, phone photos and "unknown bird" entries to identify later
  • Birding mode records your real start time and duration, or sessions are suggested afterwards from your entries
  • Checklist review: protocol, completeness and counts, with eBird's rules checked
  • eBird import files, plus a record of what you have exported and submitted
  • Backups and trip files you can move between your phone, tablet and computer

How it works with eBird and Merlin

eBird has no public way for other apps to submit checklists, so BirdTally never signs in to eBird or submits for you. It makes an eBird import file; you upload it at ebird.org (Submit → Import Data) and add photos there. Merlin is a separate app: you can note what Merlin suggested beside your own identification. BirdTally is not affiliated with the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Privacy

Your notebook stays on your device. BirdTally has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It connects to the internet only to download the eBird species list, and to download a regional species list if you add your own eBird API key. Nothing is published unless you upload it yourself.
