BirdTally Support

Last updated: 23 November 2026

BirdTally is an offline bird notebook for photographers: log birds in a few taps with no signal, then turn your birding sessions into eBird import files. Your notebook stays on your device unless you send or copy it somewhere yourself. There are no accounts.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. Please say which phone or computer and which version of iOS, Android or your browser you use, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps. Never post your backup file, and remove exact locations of sensitive species from anything you share.

Common questions

How do I get ready for a trip with no signal?

On Wi-Fi, open Setup, tick the name languages you want (for example te reo Māori) and tap Download species names. When the Offline check shows ticks for species names and offline use, BirdTally works with no connection.

What is Birding mode?

Turn Birding mode on when you start birding and off when you stop. BirdTally then knows your real start time and duration, which eBird needs for Stationary and Traveling checklists. If you just add birds with Birding mode off, BirdTally suggests birding sessions afterwards and you choose the Incidental protocol unless you know your real effort.

Does BirdTally submit checklists to eBird?

No. eBird has no public way for other apps to submit checklists. BirdTally makes an eBird import file. On ebird.org choose Submit › Import Data › eBird Record Format, upload the file, fix any unmatched species or locations and click Apply Fixes. Then paste the checklist link into BirdTally and tap Confirm submitted.

Why isn't my count the total of my entries?

Several entries of one species may be the same bird, so BirdTally uses the largest single count. If they were different birds, type the total in the checklist review.

How do I add my photos to eBird?

Photos can't go in an eBird import file. Once eBird shows your checklist, open it on ebird.org and use Add Media. Photos must be from the same date and place as the checklist.

How do I keep my notebook safe, or move it to another device?

In Setup › Backup and moving data, tap Save backup file and keep it in Files, iCloud Drive or on a computer. On the other device, use Import / merge a file. If the same sighting was changed on both devices, BirdTally keeps both versions and asks you which to keep.

Can I use Merlin with BirdTally?

Yes, side by side. Merlin has no way to share its results with other apps, so type Merlin's suggestion into the entry yourself. BirdTally is not connected with or endorsed by the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.
