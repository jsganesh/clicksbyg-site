PhilaTally Support

Last updated: 19 November 2026

PhilaTally is an iPhone and iPad app for stamp collectors: keep your collection, check "do I have it?" while buying, fill checklists and keep a want list. Your collection stays on your device unless you send or copy it somewhere yourself, such as a backup or a shared checklist. There are no accounts.

Contact

Email philatallypro@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps. Never send your full backup unless asked.

Common questions

How do I keep my collection safe?

Make a full backup now and then: Tools › Backup & Export. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive or a computer. A backup with photos holds everything: stamps, photos, checklists, wants and provenance documents.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

If you restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, PhilaTally comes across with it. Otherwise, make a full backup in PhilaTally on the old iPhone, install PhilaTally on the new one, and use Tools › Backup & Export › Restore. Restoring never needs PhilaTally Pro.

What does PhilaTally Pro do?

PhilaTally is free for up to 100 of your own stamps (demo stamps don't count). PhilaTally Pro is a one-off purchase that removes the limit and is shared with your Family Sharing group. Nothing is ever locked or hidden if you go over 100, for example after a restore.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it.

Open Tools › PhilaTally Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I share a checklist?

Open the checklist and tap Share. PhilaTally makes a .ptally file you can send by Messages, Mail or AirDrop. Another PhilaTally user taps it to import, or to update an earlier copy.

The colours in the Colour guide don't match my stamp.

Screen colours are approximate and vary between devices. The Colour guide is a guide to colour names, not a way to identify a catalogue shade.

Are the sites in Resources & Help part of PhilaTally?

No. They open in your browser, and PhilaTally isn't connected with or endorsed by them. Nothing from your collection is sent to them.

Privacy

PhilaTally collects no data about you. See the privacy policy.
