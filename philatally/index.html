PhilaTally

Your stamp collection, on iPhone and iPad.

Photograph your stamps or add them by hand, then browse and tidy the collection. At a fair or an auction, check "do I have it?" in a few taps. Work through checklists and keep a want list, and learn about each stamp as you go.

Coming to the App Store.

What it does

  • Add stamps from the camera, your photo library, or by hand
  • "Do I have it?" search while you're buying
  • Checklists, a want list and duplicates
  • Share a checklist with another collector as a single file
  • A colour guide to stamp colour names
  • Full backups you save wherever you like

Free, with one upgrade

PhilaTally is free for up to 100 of your own stamps. PhilaTally Pro is a one-off purchase that removes the limit and is shared with your Family Sharing group. Nothing is locked or hidden if you go over 100.

Private by design

No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics. Your collection stays on your device unless you send or copy it somewhere yourself.
