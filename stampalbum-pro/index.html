StampAlbum Pro

Design and print stamp album pages.

Lay out stamps and captions on a page by dragging them, or describe the page in plain text, and print it at true size. Enter each stamp's catalogue size and the frame is drawn around it, ready for mounts.

Download for Mac Windows (experimental)

Free. Alpha software. The Mac app needs an Apple-silicon Mac (M1 or later) and is signed and notarised by Apple. The Windows build is not signed yet, so Windows warns the first time you open it. All releases and notes.

What it does

  • Stamp shapes from rectangle to triangle, diamond and octagon
  • Frames drawn 1 mm clear of the stamp, captions above and below
  • Plain borders for exhibition pages, decorative ones for personal albums
  • Eight starter templates, from a single stamp to a long set
  • A text view of the page for precise or bulk editing, compatible with AlbumEasy-style files
  • Export to PDF, PNG and SVG, plus a print calibration page

Privacy

StampAlbum Pro runs entirely on your computer. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your albums or anything else over the internet. Your albums are files you save and keep.

Support

Found a problem or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. Please say which version and operating system you use.
