Prints & licensing

Fine-art prints

Prints are made on archival matte or lustre paper with pigment inks, and shipped rolled or framed. Where a photograph has a "Buy a print" button that opens a shop page, you can order directly; otherwise send an enquiry and I'll reply with sizes and prices.

Licensing

Photographs can be licensed for editorial use (articles, books, education), personal projects, and commercial use (advertising, packaging, products). Fees depend on the use, the audience size and the term. A licence is granted only once it is agreed in writing and paid.

What isn't allowed without a licence

  • Downloading, copying or re-posting the photographs, including with credit
  • Using them to train or prompt AI image models
  • Printing them, or selling products that use them

Sharing a link to a page on this site is always welcome.
