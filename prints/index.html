Ordering within New Zealand

  1. Choose a photograph below and press Order this print. An email opens with the details to fill in.

  3. Once payment arrives, your print is made and sent to you.

Ordering from outside New Zealand

The prices above don't include packaging and international postage, which depend on the size, the frame and where it's going.

  1. Press Ask for a delivered price under the photograph, or the button below for more than one print. Include your country and delivery address.
  2. I reply with a total that includes packaging and tracked postage, and send a PayPal invoice, which you can pay with PayPal or by card.
  3. Once the invoice is paid, your print is made and posted.
